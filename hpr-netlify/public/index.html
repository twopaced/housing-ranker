Model Transparency

  • Assumption: Past 3–10 year price growth and volatility are used as a proxy for future opportunity and risk; states are scored relative to each other, not against an absolute standard.
  • Limitation: State-level data lags and gets revised, and it hides metro and neighborhood differences; median listing price is a budget filter, not a purchase price.
  • Does NOT mean: A high score is not a prediction of returns, rent yield, or a buy signal. Rents, taxes, insurance, and financing costs are excluded. This is not financial advice.
Data: FRED API series STHPI (house price index), PCPI (per-capita income), MEDLISPRI (median listing price), all 50 states + DC.
